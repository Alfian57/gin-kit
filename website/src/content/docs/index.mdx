GinKit is a small, opinionated layer around [Gin](https://gin-gonic.com/). It
gives a new service a deliberate starting point while keeping your handlers,
domains, services, and repositories in your repository.

## Pick your starting point

- **Framework edition** — a thin application that imports GinKit’s versioned
  runtime. Generic lifecycle and HTTP policy stay out of your repository.
- **Starter edition** — a standalone, source-visible project for teams that
  want every infrastructure detail in their own module.

Both editions are generated by the same CLI and keep business code yours.
