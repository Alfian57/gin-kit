# Code of Conduct

gin-kit follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Participants in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project
space are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and ask questions before escalating.
- Welcome newcomers and different levels of experience.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope and direction gracefully.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sustained disruption,
and bad-faith behavior are not welcome anywhere in the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through
[GitHub's report abuse form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) or by
opening a security advisory contact via the repository's Security tab.
Maintainers will review reports confidentially and may warn, moderate, or ban
participants at their discretion.
